ENTERPRISE SALES INTELLIGENCE - WEBSITE QUICK START

1. Extract the ZIP to a folder you can write to.
2. Double-click RUN_WEBSITE.bat.
3. Wait for Streamlit to start, then open http://localhost:8501 in Chrome.
4. Select Upload Sales CSV.
5. Choose your CSV and click Run Sales Analysis.
6. Review the Dashboard and all analysis pages. Download outputs from the
   Cleaned Data, Rejected Records, Anomaly Detection, and Management Report pages.
7. Keep the terminal window open while using the website.

To stop the website, focus the terminal and press Ctrl+C.

Uploaded CSV files are processed separately from sample CSVs in data/raw.
